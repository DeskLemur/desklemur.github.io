02 / DIRECT PLASTICITY

Can AI grow
through experience?

ONGOING RESEARCH

Direct Plasticity explores more adaptive AI: how experience might support useful memory, continuity, and improvement over time.

We are developing this direction in stages. DPMS v1 provides an input-token measurement; later stages need their own evaluation.
